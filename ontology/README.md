# ontology

The evolving semantic lexicon.

Words enter from use. The lexicon records those words and the situations they name.

Matching and generalized object strategy may be adapted from Notention later, where they fit.

**Assent**

- An offer is a proposal made by an identity. It may receive zero or more acceptance records.
- An acceptance is a record of assent to an offer, and it references that offer.
- An agreement is the terms and scope to which the parties assent. The scope names the parties.
- A contract is an agreement for which all required parties have assented.
- Incomplete assent, withdrawn assent, conflicting terms, and disputed claims are representable. A contract is the agreement once every party its scope requires has assented.

Revision is unsettled. A private object, still on the point of contact, can be changed freely. A published object is hard to change, because other identities may already refer to it. How the network disposes of unwanted objects is open.
