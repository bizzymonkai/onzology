# ontology

The evolving semantic lexicon.

Words enter from use. The lexicon records those words and the situations they name.

Matching and generalized object strategy may be adapted from Notention later, where they fit.

**Assent**

- An offer may receive zero or more acceptance records.
- An agreement becomes mutually assented to only when every required party has assented to the same agreement, or to its terms.
- Incomplete assent, withdrawn assent, conflicting terms, and disputed claims are representable. A completed contract is mutual assent by every required party.

Revision is unsettled. A private object, still on the point of contact, can be changed freely. A published object is hard to change, because other identities may already refer to it. How the network disposes of unwanted objects is open.
