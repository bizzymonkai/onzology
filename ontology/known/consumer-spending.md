# Consumer spending

A known vocabulary. Source: [Consumer Spending Ontology for P2P Credit Networks, version 1.0](https://monkeypanel.com/ontology.html) (MonkeyPanel).

It classifies what a situation is about: food, shelter, a ride, a gift. It is not the Onzology model, and it does not decide who owes whom.

## Kept

The categories below, and the cross-cutting words for time, social context, and risk. A record may use them. A record may ignore them. An unmatched word is allowed. This list is not closed, and it does not have to classify every situation.

## Not kept

- Creditor and debtor as facts created by logging an expense. Naming another identity is not that identity's assent. An offer may receive zero acceptances. Mutual assent still requires every required party.
- Settlement terms, a priority class, and a dispute-eligibility flag as fields every record must carry. A disputed claim can be published even if someone called the situation non-disputable.
- Trust scores, penalties, staked mediators, and a ruling that the loser pays. Those are rulers.
- A miscellaneous bin that waits on manual review. Unclassified is a normal state.
- The page's random lifecycle, which opens a debt when one party logs an expense and invites the others. That invitation is a claim. It is not an agreement.

The source skips section 5. That gap is left as it was.

# Primary categories

## 2.1 SUSTENANCE & NUTRITION

*All consumables ingested for survival, pleasure, or social bonding*

### 2.1.1 Food Acquisition

- **Groceries.** Unprepared raw ingredients, pantry staples, beverages for home consumption
- **Meal Kits.** Pre-portioned ingredients with preparation instructions
- **Specialty Foods.** Dietary-specific (gluten-free, keto, halal, organic certifications)

### 2.1.2 Prepared Consumption

- **Quick Service.** Fast food, street food, food trucks, grab-and-go
- **Casual Dining.** Sit-down restaurants, cafes, diners (check splitting scenarios)
- **Fine Dining.** Tasting menus, sommelier services, private dining rooms
- **Ghost Kitchens.** Delivery-only restaurant concepts

### 2.1.3 Beverages (Alcoholic & Non-Alcoholic)

- **Coffee & Tea.** Specialty coffee shops, bubble tea, afternoon tea services
- **Alcohol.** Bars, pubs, wine bars, brewery taprooms, bottle service
- **Smoothies & Juices.** Health-focused beverage bars

### 2.1.4 Subscriptions & Recurring

- **Meal Delivery.** Weekly recipe boxes, prepared meal subscriptions
- **CSA/Farm Shares.** Community supported agriculture boxes

## 2.2 SHELTER & HABITATION

*Physical space occupancy and modification*

### 2.2.1 Residential Payments

- **Rent.** Primary residence, subletting, room rentals, Airbnb splits
- **Mortgage Contributions.** Housemate contributions to mortgage payments
- **Security Deposits.** Move-in costs, last month's rent prepayment

### 2.2.2 Utilities & Infrastructure

- **Electricity.** Grid power, solar buyback credits
- **Water & Sewer.** Municipal services, well maintenance splits
- **Gas.** Natural gas, propane delivery
- **Trash & Waste.** Collection fees, special disposal (electronics, hazardous)
- **Internet/Broadband.** Cable, fiber, satellite, mobile hotspots
- **Telecommunications.** Landline, mobile family plan splits

### 2.2.3 Maintenance & Supplies

- **Cleaning Services.** Housekeepers, carpet cleaning, window washing
- **Maintenance.** HVAC repair, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair
- **Supplies.** Cleaning products, light bulbs, batteries, hardware
- **Lawn & Garden.** Landscaping, snow removal, pest control

### 2.2.4 Furniture & Furnishings

- **Major Furniture.** Sofas, beds, dining sets (group purchases)
- **Appliances.** Refrigerators, washers, small appliances
- **Decor.** Art, rugs, curtains, throw pillows (shared aesthetic purchases)

## 2.3 TRANSPORTATION & MOBILITY

*Movement of persons and goods from origin to destination*

### 2.3.1 Vehicle Operations

- **Fuel.** Gasoline, diesel, electric charging (including supercharging splits)
- **Maintenance.** Oil changes, tire rotation, brake service
- **Repairs.** Mechanical breakdowns, body work, glass replacement
- **Insurance.** Premium splits, deductible reimbursements
- **Parking.** Meter fees, garage rentals, parking tickets, valet

### 2.3.2 Vehicle Acquisition

- **Lease Payments.** Monthly lease splits
- **Loan Payments.** Auto loan contributions
- **Shared Ownership.** Fractional ownership arrangements

### 2.3.3 Alternative Transportation

- **Rideshare.** Uber, Lyft, taxi services (group ride splits)
- **Public Transit.** Bus, subway, train, ferry passes and single fares
- **Micromobility.** Bike shares, e-scooter rentals, e-bike purchases
- **Carpooling.** Gas money reimbursements, toll splits

### 2.3.4 Long-Distance Travel

- **Airfare.** Commercial flights, charter services, baggage fees
- **Rail.** Amtrak, high-speed rail, sleeper cars
- **Bus.** Greyhound, Megabus, BoltBus
- **Sea.** Ferries, cruises (group cabin bookings)

## 2.4 HEALTHCARE & WELLNESS

*Maintenance and restoration of physical and mental health*

### 2.4.1 Medical Services

- **Primary Care.** Doctor visits, annual physicals, vaccinations
- **Specialist Care.** Cardiology, dermatology, orthopedics, etc.
- **Emergency Services.** ER visits, urgent care, ambulance transport
- **Surgical Procedures.** Inpatient and outpatient surgeries
- **Dental.** Cleanings, fillings, orthodontics, oral surgery
- **Vision.** Eye exams, glasses, contact lenses, LASIK

### 2.4.2 Mental Health

- **Therapy.** Individual counseling, couples therapy, family therapy
- **Psychiatry.** Medication management, psychiatric evaluations
- **Support Services.** Life coaching, career counseling, support groups

### 2.4.3 Wellness & Prevention

- **Gym Memberships.** Fitness centers, CrossFit boxes, yoga studios
- **Personal Training.** One-on-one fitness instruction
- **Alternative Medicine.** Acupuncture, chiropractic, massage therapy
- **Nutrition.** Dietitian services, supplement subscriptions
- **Spa Services.** Facials, massages, saunas, wellness retreats

### 2.4.4 Pharmaceuticals & Supplies

- **Prescriptions.** Medication co-pays, full-price medications
- **Over-the-Counter.** Pain relievers, cold medicine, first aid supplies
- **Medical Devices.** CPAP machines, blood glucose monitors, wheelchairs

# Cross-cutting descriptions

Optional. Not required fields.

## 3.1 Temporal Characteristics

- **Recurring.** Weekly, monthly, annual subscriptions
- **One-Time.** Single purchase, non-repeating
- **Seasonal.** Holiday-specific, weather-dependent, tax season
- **Emergency.** Unplanned, urgent, crisis-driven

## 3.2 Social Context

- **Group Expense.** Split among multiple parties (even or uneven)
- **Gift.** Third-party beneficiary, giver not receiver
- **Reimbursement.** Repayment for previously fronted expense
- **Allowance.** Regular transfer to dependent

## 3.3 Risk & Dispute Profile

- **Disputable.** High chargeback risk (retail, services)
- **Non-Disputable.** Tips, gifts, gambling, certain adult services
- **High-Value.** Above threshold requiring documentation
- **Micro-Transaction.** Below $10, often cash-equivalent

# Edge cases in the source

Kept as words a peer might use. Onzology does not adopt a legality classifier, and it does not require any of these. The source's line "ensure zero unclassified transactions" is rejected. Unclassified is allowed.

## 4.1 Cryptocurrency & Digital Assets

- **NFT Purchases.** Digital art, collectibles, virtual real estate
- **Crypto Trading.** Coin purchases, exchange fees, gas fees
- **Virtual Goods.** Game skins, virtual currency, digital gifts

## 4.2 Illegal or Gray Market

- **Cash Businesses.** Undocumented services, informal labor
- **Vice Industries.** Legal cannabis, alcohol (where restricted), adult entertainment
- **Underground Economy.** Unreported income payments (documented as "miscellaneous services")

## 4.3 Meta-Categories

- **Miscellaneous.** Any transaction defying above categories (requires manual review flag)
- **Adjustment.** Corrections, refunds, currency conversion adjustments, fees
- **Inter-Node Transfers.** Movement between accounts of same entity (not spending)

# What the source claimed to cover

The author's checklist, not a requirement of this repository.

- **✅ Maslow's Hierarchy.** Physiological (food, shelter) through self-actualization (education, hobbies)
- **✅ Life Stages.** Childhood (dependents) through elder care
- **✅ Economic Sectors.** Primary (food) through quaternary (knowledge work)
- **✅ Temporal Coverage.** Emergency through luxury, recurring through one-time
- **✅ Social Coverage.** Individual through group, intimate partners through strangers
- **✅ Legal Coverage.** Fully documented through gray market
- **✅ Digital/Physical.** NFTs through farmers market cash purchases
